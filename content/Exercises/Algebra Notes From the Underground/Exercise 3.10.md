What is $(a +b)(a - b)$ in a ring? What is $(a b)^3$ 

>[!answer]-
> $(a + b)(a - b) = (a + b) a - (a + b)b = a^2 + b a - a b - b^2$. It is not necessarily $a^2 - b^2$ since the multiplication is not commutative.
> 
> $(a b)^3 = a b a a b a b$. It is not necessarily $a^3 b^3$ since the multiplication is not commutative.
