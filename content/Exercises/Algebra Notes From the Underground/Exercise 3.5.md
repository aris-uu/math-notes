Let $R$ be a ring, and assume that $0 = 1$ in $R$. Prove that $R$ consists of a single element.

>[!answer]-
> Since $0$ is in $R$, it is nonempty and thus has at least one element. We will prove that it has at most one element. Let $a \in R$. Then since $0 = 1$, $a = a \cdot 1 = a \cdot 0 = 0$. Thus, every element of $R$ is $0$. Therefore, $R$ only consists of a single element. $\blacksquare$
