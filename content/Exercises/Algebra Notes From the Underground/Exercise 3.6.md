Let $R$ be a ring. Prove that $\forall a \in R, (-1) \cdot a = -a$.

>[!answer]-
> Let $R$ be a ring, and $a \in R$. Then,
> $$
> 0 = 0 \cdot a = (1+ (-1)) \cdot a  = 1 \cdot a + (-1) \cdot a = a + (-1) \cdot a
> $$
> Since multiplicative inverses are unique, then $(-1) \cdot a = -a$. $\blacksquare$
